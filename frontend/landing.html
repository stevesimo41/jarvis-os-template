How It Works

Three steps to an AI-powered pipeline

JARVIS automates the research, preparation, and outreach — while you stay in the loop.

1

JARVIS scans the web

Market Pulse monitors job boards, news, and business signals in your area. It finds companies that need what you sell — before they even know they need it.

2

It prepares everything for you

For each opportunity, JARVIS enriches the prospect: finds emails, phone numbers, executive names, and company details. Then it presents everything for your review.

3

You approve, it executes

Click approve and JARVIS runs your outreach cadence — email, contact forms, follow-ups. Nothing goes out without your say-so. You're always in control.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Modular agents, real-time data, and a command center designed for how you actually work.

📡

Market Pulse

Scans job postings, business news, and market signals in your target area. Surfaces revenue opportunities you'd miss manually.

🔍

Prospect Enrichment

Finds emails, phone numbers, executive names, and LinkedIn profiles from any company website. No manual research needed.

📝

Contact Form Detection

Auto-detects and submits to website contact forms (WordPress, Gravity Forms, HubSpot, and more). CAPTCHA detection built in.

✅

Approval Governance

Nothing goes out without your approval. Every action is queued, explained, and waiting for your click. You're always in control.

Multi-LLM Support

Works with Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, or run locally with Ollama. No LLM? No problem — the rule engine handles it.

📊

CRM Pipeline

Connect Google Sheets, Airtable, or any database. Or use the built-in approval queue standalone. Your data stays yours.
