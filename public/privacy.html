Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What JARVIS OS Is

JARVIS OS is a self-hosted personal AI operating system. When you run JARVIS OS on your own machine, your data never leaves your device. There is no central server collecting your information.

Data Storage

All data generated by JARVIS OS — including contacts, approvals, agent activity, and configuration — is stored locally on your machine in JSON files. No data is transmitted to external servers unless you explicitly configure an integration (such as a CRM, email provider, or LLM API).

Third-Party Integrations

If you connect JARVIS OS to third-party services (Google Sheets, email providers, LLM APIs, Lemon Squeezy), those services have their own privacy policies. JARVIS OS only sends data to these services when you explicitly configure and authorize the connection.

Lemon Squeezy

If you purchase JARVIS OS Pro or Business through Lemon Squeezy, Lemon Squeezy processes your payment and collects billing information per their own privacy policy. JARVIS OS receives order confirmation via webhook but does not store your payment details.

Cookies and Sessions

JARVIS OS uses session cookies for authentication when running its web interface. These cookies are stored locally and are not used for tracking.

Open Source

JARVIS OS is open source under the AGPL v3 license. You can review the code to verify exactly what data is collected and how it is used.
